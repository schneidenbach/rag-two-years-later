# Facilities Operations Monthly Review - Minutes, June 2026

## June 2026 Facilities Operations review meeting details and attendance

Meeting date: June 17, 2026, 1:00 pm to 2:30 pm, North Campus Central Plant conference room and virtual.
Chair: Corinne Ashby, Director of Facilities Operations.
Present: Glen Harlow (Plant Operations Manager), Nolan Achterberg (Plumbing Supervisor), Wendell Ruiz (General Maintenance Supervisor), Imelda Carrow (HVAC Supervisor), Jun Takeda (Facilities Projects Manager), Tobias Lund (Supply Chain, MRO Category Manager), Bridget Saunders (Accreditation and Regulatory Readiness), Charlotte Engel (Nursing Operations, North Campus).
Recorder: Facilities administrative coordinator.
The May 2026 minutes were approved as distributed.

## June 2026 review of work order key performance indicators

Glen Harlow presented May work order performance. Overall system volume was in line with seasonal expectations, with North Campus continuing to carry the highest volume of any campus. P3 and P4 completion compliance remained at target. P1 response compliance was at target for all trades. The plumbing trade showed declining P2 completion compliance, concentrated at North Campus. The CMMS dashboard has been updated to show time in "Awaiting Parts" status by trade and building.

## June 2026 discussion of the Tower B plumbing backlog

Nolan Achterberg reported a growing plumbing work order backlog in North Campus Tower B since March, primarily sensor flush valves, mixing valves, and medical gas outlet repairs on inpatient units, including the ICUs. Most of the aged Tower B plumbing work orders are on hold in "Awaiting Parts" status for Aqualine SV-200 solenoid modules and MedGuard MG-4 O2 outlet latch assemblies ordered from ACME Mechanical Supply. Several patient rooms have been taken out of service pending parts. Nolan noted that under the prior Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement these parts were pulled from the consignment cage the same day, and that Facilities was not consulted on the Exhibit B specialty item list in the ACME agreement before it was signed.

Tobias Lund (Supply Chain) stated that the ACME delays are **temporary**, attributed to a manufacturer allocation issue at Aqualine and to ACME establishing new manufacturer accounts. Supply Chain expects lead times to normalize by the end of the summer. The first ACME quarterly business review was held in May 2026; Supply Chain reported that fill rate reporting by Exhibit was not yet available from ACME at that time.

Charlotte Engel (Nursing) reported that out-of-service rooms in Tower B are affecting patient flow and ED boarding, and asked for estimated return-to-service dates for each room.

## June 2026 discussion of early experience with after-hours SOP FAC-OPS-014 Rev 4

Wendell Ruiz reported that General Maintenance night technicians are responding to more after-hours plumbing calls since SOP FAC-OPS-014 Revision 4 took effect on 2026-04-01. Many of these calls involve flush valves, mixing valves, or medical gas outlets that GM technicians are not licensed to repair, so the GM technician makes the area safe and the ticket waits for reassignment to a plumber at the start of day shift. Corinne Ashby noted that Rev 4 is tied to the FY26 overtime reduction target of 18% and that overtime data for April and May shows a reduction in trade callouts. The group agreed the effect of Rev 4 on plumbing completion times should be reviewed.

## June 2026 HVAC summer cooling load update

Imelda Carrow reported that the North Campus central chilled water plant entered summer mode on May 28. Chiller 3 is out of service for a scheduled compressor rebuild through early July, leaving N+1 redundancy only during peak afternoons. Hot-room complaints in Tower A and Riverside Hospital are elevated, consistent with prior summers. Cooling tower water treatment is on schedule and Legionella sampling for the cooling towers was within limits.

## June 2026 Tower A elevator modernization update

Jun Takeda reported that the Tower A elevator modernization project is on schedule. Car 2 returned to service on June 3 after controller and machine replacement. Car 3 is out of service for modernization through mid-August. Patient transport has been asked to use the Tower A/Tower B connector on floor 2 during peak periods. The elevator contractor's next phase requires a shaft ICRA permit, which Infection Prevention has approved.
