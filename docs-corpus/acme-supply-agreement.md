# Plumbing and Mechanical Parts Supply Agreement between Lakeshore Health and ACME Mechanical Supply

## §1 Parties and term of the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§1.1 This Plumbing and Mechanical Parts Supply Agreement, agreement no. LH-SC-2026-017 (the "Agreement"), is entered into between Lakeshore Health ("Lakeshore") and ACME Mechanical Supply ("ACME").

§1.2 The Agreement is effective 2026-02-24 and continues for an initial term of three (3) years, renewing for successive one-year terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal.

§1.3 ACME is the primary supplier of plumbing repair parts, fixtures, and related mechanical maintenance, repair, and operations (MRO) items for all Lakeshore campuses: North Campus, South Campus, and Riverside Campus. This Agreement replaces the prior plumbing parts supply arrangement with Great Lakes Plumbing Supply.

## §2 Definitions used in the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§2.1 "Stocked Item" means any item listed in Exhibit A of this Agreement, which ACME maintains in inventory at its regional distribution center.

§2.2 "Non-Stocked Specialty Item" means any item listed in Exhibit B of this Agreement, which ACME procures from the manufacturer on a per-order basis.

§2.3 "Business Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

§2.4 "Fill Rate" means the percentage of order lines delivered complete within the applicable lead time stated in §4, measured monthly.

## §3 Ordering under the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§3.1 Lakeshore will place orders through its enterprise resource planning system or the ACME customer portal. Orders received by ACME by 2:00 pm local time are deemed received that Business Day.

§3.2 Facilities Operations technicians may place orders directly against this Agreement up to their delegated purchasing limit.

## §4 Delivery lead times, consignment, and backorders under the ACME agreement

§4.1 **Stocked items.** ACME shall deliver Stocked Items (Exhibit A) to the designated Lakeshore campus receiving dock within two (2) Business Days of order receipt.

§4.2 **Non-stocked specialty items.** For Non-Stocked Specialty Items listed in Exhibit B, ACME shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver within ten (10) Business Days of order receipt. Lead times for Exhibit B items are targets and are dependent on manufacturer availability.

§4.3 **No consignment.** No consignment inventory, vendor-managed inventory, or on-site stock is provided under this Agreement. Lakeshore is responsible for any safety stock it elects to purchase and hold.

§4.4 **Backorders.** If any ordered item is backordered, ACME shall notify Lakeshore within two (2) Business Days of order receipt and provide a committed estimated delivery date (ETA). If a backorder exceeds fifteen (15) Business Days from order receipt, Lakeshore may source the item from another supplier and charge ACME the difference between the alternate supplier's price and the Agreement price.

§4.5 **Expedited delivery.** Lakeshore may request expedited delivery of any item at Lakeshore's cost, except as provided in §7.1.

## §5 Pricing under the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§5.1 Prices for Exhibit A and Exhibit B items are fixed for the first twelve (12) months of the term and thereafter may be adjusted annually by no more than the published producer price index for plumbing fixtures.

§5.2 Pricing under this Agreement reflects the Lakeshore vendor consolidation initiative and represents an average unit cost reduction of approximately 11% compared with Lakeshore's prior plumbing parts pricing.

## §6 Returns and warranty under the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§6.1 Stocked Items may be returned unused within sixty (60) days without restocking fee. Non-Stocked Specialty Items are non-returnable unless defective.

§6.2 ACME passes through all manufacturer warranties. Defective parts will be replaced at no charge under the same lead times as §4.

## §7 Service levels, reporting, and remedies under the ACME agreement

§7.1 **Fill rate service level.** ACME shall maintain a monthly Fill Rate of not less than 95%. If the Fill Rate falls below 95% for two (2) consecutive months, Lakeshore may (a) require that subsequent orders be shipped by expedited freight at ACME's cost until the Fill Rate is restored, and (b) require ACME to deliver a written corrective action plan within ten (10) Business Days of Lakeshore's request.

§7.2 **Reporting.** ACME shall provide a monthly service report to Lakeshore Supply Chain showing Fill Rate overall and by Exhibit, open backorders, and average days to deliver.

§7.3 **Quarterly business review.** The parties shall hold a quarterly business review (QBR) to review service performance, pricing, open issues, and continuous improvement opportunities. Lakeshore Supply Chain chairs the QBR and may invite operating departments.

## §8 through §11 general terms of the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§8 Insurance: ACME maintains commercial general liability and product liability coverage as required by Lakeshore vendor policy. §9 Confidentiality: Each party protects the other's confidential information. §10 Compliance: ACME complies with Lakeshore vendor credentialing requirements for any personnel entering Lakeshore facilities. §11 Notices: Notices are delivered to Lakeshore Supply Chain contract administration.

## §12 Termination of the ACME Mechanical Supply agreement

§12.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience at the end of the initial term with ninety (90) days' written notice.

§12.2 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other party materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure the breach within sixty (60) days after written notice describing the breach. Repeated failure to meet the service level in §7.1 constitutes a material breach.
