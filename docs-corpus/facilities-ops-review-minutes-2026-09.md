# Facilities Operations Monthly Review - Minutes, September 2026

## September 2026 Facilities Operations review meeting details and attendance

Meeting date: September 16, 2026, 1:00 pm to 2:45 pm, North Campus Central Plant conference room and virtual.
Chair: Corinne Ashby, Director of Facilities Operations.
Present: Glen Harlow (Plant Operations Manager), Nolan Achterberg (Plumbing Supervisor), Wendell Ruiz (General Maintenance Supervisor), Imelda Carrow (HVAC Supervisor), Jun Takeda (Facilities Projects Manager), Tobias Lund (Supply Chain, MRO Category Manager), Renata Vos (Finance, FP&A), Charlotte Engel (Nursing Operations, North Campus).
Recorder: Facilities administrative coordinator.
The August 2026 minutes were approved as distributed.

## September 2026 review of work order key performance indicators

Glen Harlow presented August work order performance. Electrical, HVAC, carpentry, and General Maintenance met completion targets for all priorities. Plumbing P1 and P2 completion compliance declined again in August and remains the lowest of any trade, with the shortfall concentrated in North Campus Tower B and in the medical gas, backflow, and fixture valve categories. Average days in "Awaiting Parts" status for Tower B plumbing work orders remains well above other buildings. Plumbing work orders in other buildings, which use commodity parts, are closing within target.

## September 2026 ACME Mechanical Supply fill rate on Exhibit B items

Tobias Lund presented the first ACME service report broken out by Exhibit. ACME's fill rate on **Exhibit B items was 71% in July and 68% in August 2026**. Exhibit A items were above 98% in both months. ACME attributes the Exhibit B shortfall to continued manufacturer allocation for Aqualine and MedGuard parts. Some Tower B orders have been open for several weeks.

Nolan Achterberg asked what options Lakeshore has with ACME. Tobias Lund said Supply Chain will follow up with Contract Administration and raise the topic with the ACME account team at the next vendor meeting. Corinne Ashby requested that Facilities be invited to that meeting.

Renata Vos asked Facilities to provide a count of Tower B room-days out of service attributable to parts delays for the Finance capacity report.

## September 2026 after-hours General Maintenance plumbing callbacks

Wendell Ruiz reported that overnight General Maintenance callouts for plumbing issues have continued to rise each month since April. Most are P2 Urgent calls for toilets and flush valves, mixing valves, and medical gas outlets on Tower B inpatient units. Under SOP FAC-OPS-014 Rev 4, these calls route to the GM on-call first; GM technicians are not licensed for flushometer or sensor valve, medical gas, or backflow repair, so they make the area safe and the ticket is reassigned to a plumber at the start of the next day shift. Charge nurses have reported repeated overnight visits to the same room without repair. Charlotte Engel noted that Nursing understood from prior practice (Rev 3) that P2 plumbing calls would page the on-call plumber.

Action item AI-2026-06-04 (review effect of SOP FAC-OPS-014 Rev 4 on after-hours plumbing response and completion times) remains **open**. The review has not started because the General Maintenance Supervisor position was covered on an interim basis in July and August. Corinne Ashby noted the review must also consider the FY26 overtime reduction target of 18%.

## September 2026 HVAC summer cooling load recap

Imelda Carrow reported that summer cooling season closed without loss of cooling to any critical area. Chiller 3 returned to service on July 9 after compressor rebuild. Hot-room complaints peaked in late July during the regional heat event and declined through August. The team will begin fall changeover of the Riverside Hospital boilers in October and will schedule air handler coil cleaning for Tower A in November.

## September 2026 Tower A elevator modernization update

Jun Takeda reported that Tower A elevator Car 3 returned to service on August 21, completing the second phase. The final phase (Car 1, service car) is scheduled for January through March 2027 and will require temporary relocation of the Tower A soiled linen route. The project remains within budget.
