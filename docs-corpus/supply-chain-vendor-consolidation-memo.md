# Memo: Plumbing and Mechanical Parts Vendor Consolidation

**To:** Department Directors, Facilities Operations, Finance, Environmental Services, Clinical Engineering
**From:** Malcolm Pierce, Vice President, Supply Chain
**Date:** February 9, 2026
**Subject:** Transition of plumbing parts supply from Great Lakes Plumbing Supply to ACME Mechanical Supply

## Summary of the plumbing parts vendor consolidation decision

Supply Chain has completed its FY26 review of maintenance, repair, and operations (MRO) suppliers. As part of the system-wide vendor consolidation initiative, Lakeshore Health will consolidate plumbing and mechanical parts purchasing under a single supplier, ACME Mechanical Supply. The new supply agreement, no. LH-SC-2026-017, takes effect February 24, 2026. The current Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement ends February 23, 2026, and will not be renewed.

## Expected cost savings from the ACME Mechanical Supply consolidation

Based on twelve months of purchase history, ACME pricing represents approximately 11% unit cost savings across the plumbing parts category. Additional savings are expected from reduced invoice volume, elimination of the consignment carrying charge embedded in Great Lakes pricing, and consolidated freight. Savings will be reported in the monthly Supply Chain value analysis report.

## Expected service level impact of the ACME Mechanical Supply transition

ACME will deliver stocked items within two business days to any campus receiving dock. Specialty items not carried in ACME's regional inventory will be ordered from the manufacturer with a ten business day lead time target. ACME has committed to a 95% monthly fill rate, with remedies available to Lakeshore if that level is not met. Supply Chain has reviewed ACME's delivery performance with two peer health systems in the region. **No material impact on service levels is expected** as a result of this transition.

## End of Great Lakes consignment stock at North Campus

Great Lakes Plumbing Supply currently maintains consignment inventory in the North Campus Central Plant storeroom. This consignment arrangement will end with the Great Lakes agreement on February 23, 2026, and Great Lakes will remove its inventory on that date. The ACME agreement does not include consignment or on-site stock. Supply Chain does not recommend purchasing the remaining consignment inventory, as ACME's two business day delivery for stocked items is expected to meet routine repair needs without the cost of holding inventory.

## Exhibit B specialty item classification in the ACME agreement

Items in the plumbing category were classified as either stocked (Exhibit A) or non-stocked specialty (Exhibit B) by Supply Chain category management, using ACME's stocking catalog and Lakeshore's twelve-month usage history. Exhibit B consists primarily of manufacturer-specific parts for North Campus Tower B fixtures, including Aqualine sensor flush valve components, ThermaMix mixing valves, MedGuard medical gas components, and RPZ backflow repair kits. Because these are MRO commodity items, the classification was completed within Supply Chain and was not routed for departmental review.

## Stakeholders consulted for the vendor consolidation

The following groups were consulted during the vendor evaluation: Supply Chain category management, Accounts Payable, Finance (FP&A), and Contract Administration. Facilities Operations was notified of the transition on this date and will receive ACME ordering credentials and portal training before February 24, 2026.

## Actions for departments during the ACME Mechanical Supply transition

1. Facilities Operations technicians should place plumbing orders with ACME beginning February 24, 2026, using the ACME customer portal or the ERP requisition process.
2. Any open Great Lakes orders not received by February 23, 2026, will be cancelled and re-placed with ACME by Supply Chain.
3. Questions on lead times or item availability should be directed to the Supply Chain MRO category manager.
4. Supply Chain will host the first ACME quarterly business review in May 2026.
