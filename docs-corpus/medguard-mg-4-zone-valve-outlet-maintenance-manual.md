# MedGuard MG-4 Zone Valve Box and Station Outlet Maintenance (Excerpt)

## Scope of the MedGuard MG-4 maintenance excerpt

This excerpt covers inspection and maintenance of MedGuard MG-4 medical gas zone valve boxes and MedGuard MG-4 headwall station outlets (oxygen, medical air, and vacuum) as installed in North Campus Tower B, including the Tower B intensive care units. It supplements, and does not replace, the Lakeshore medical gas program and the full MedGuard installation manual.

## Lakeshore qualified personnel rule for MedGuard MG-4 medical gas work

Under Lakeshore's internal medical gas rule, which follows nationally recognized medical gas installation practice, **only licensed medical gas installers** credentialed by Lakeshore may open an MG-4 zone valve box, operate a zone valve except in an emergency shutoff, or disassemble or repair a station outlet. General Maintenance technicians are not licensed for medical gas work under SOP FAC-OPS-014 and may not perform these tasks. After any repair that opens the system, the affected zone must be verified for cross-connection, pressure, flow, and purity before return to service, and the verification documented in the CMMS.

## MedGuard MG-4 zone valve box description and inspection

The MG-4 zone valve box houses ball-type zone valves for each gas served, with pressure gauges downstream of each valve and a breakable or removable window. Each box must be labeled with the gas, the rooms or areas served, and a caution notice. Quarterly inspection includes: confirming the box is unobstructed and visible, confirming labels are legible and correct, checking gauge readings against the expected pressure range, and checking for audible leaks. Zone valve replacement requires shutdown of the zone, coordination with Respiratory Therapy and nursing, and a written shutdown plan.

## MedGuard MG-4 zone valve replacement

A failed MG-4 zone valve (failure to close fully, stem leakage, or failed gauge port) is replaced as a complete valve assembly (MedGuard part MG4-ZV). MG-4 zone valves are specific to the MG-4 box mounting rail and extension tube dimensions; other manufacturers' zone valves will not fit the MG-4 box without box replacement, which requires wall penetration and an ICRA permit under IC-210.

## MedGuard MG-4 station outlet description and common failures

MG-4 station outlets use gas-specific indexed latch assemblies so that equipment for one gas cannot be connected to another. Common failures reported by nursing and Respiratory Therapy include an oxygen outlet that will not latch a flowmeter, an outlet that hisses or leaks when equipment is connected, and low suction at a vacuum outlet. An outlet that cannot safely hold equipment is tagged out of service.

## MedGuard MG-4 O2 outlet latch assembly replacement

The O2 outlet latch assembly can be replaced from the front of the outlet without opening the wall:

1. Confirm the replacement part is the MedGuard MG-4 O2 outlet latch assembly (MedGuard part MG4-O2LA). **The outlet latch assembly is model-specific**; latch assemblies from other MedGuard models or other manufacturers are not interchangeable and will not maintain gas-specific indexing.
2. Notify the charge nurse and Respiratory Therapy; confirm no patient is connected to the outlet.
3. Remove the faceplate. The MG-4 primary check valve closes automatically when the latch assembly is removed.
4. Remove and replace the latch assembly and seals.
5. Reinstall the faceplate and perform a connector function test, a leak test, and a flow test.
6. Document the test results in the CMMS work order before returning the outlet to service.

## MedGuard MG-4 vacuum outlet rebuild

Low suction at an MG-4 vacuum outlet with normal zone vacuum pressure indicates a worn valve seat or seal. Rebuild using the MedGuard MG-4 vacuum outlet rebuild kit (MedGuard part MG4-VRK), then verify flow at the outlet.

## MedGuard MG-4 spare parts recommendation

MedGuard recommends that hospitals maintain on-site spares of zone valves for each gas and pipe size in use, O2 outlet latch assemblies, and vacuum rebuild kits, because MG-4 parts are distributed only through authorized medical gas distributors and an outlet awaiting parts results in the patient bed position being unusable.
