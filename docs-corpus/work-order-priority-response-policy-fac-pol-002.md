# Policy FAC-POL-002: Work Order Priority and Response

## Purpose and scope of the work order priority policy FAC-POL-002

This policy establishes the priority levels used for all Facilities Operations corrective maintenance work orders at Lakeshore Health, and the response and completion time targets associated with each priority. It applies to all campuses and buildings and to all trades: plumbing, electrical, HVAC, carpentry, and General Maintenance. Priority is assigned by the Facilities service desk at intake and may be changed by the responding technician or supervisor based on field conditions.

## Work order priority levels and response targets

| Priority | Name | Respond within | Complete within |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Emergency | 30 minutes | 24 hours |
| P2 | Urgent | 4 hours | 72 hours |
| P3 | Routine | 1 business day | 10 business days |
| P4 | Scheduled | Per PM calendar | Per PM calendar |

"Respond" means a qualified technician has arrived at the location and begun assessment. "Complete" means the condition is corrected and the work order is closed in the CMMS, or the work order is converted to a project with an approved plan.

## Definition of P1 Emergency work orders

A P1 Emergency is a condition that presents an immediate threat to life safety, patient care, or the building, or that causes loss of a critical utility. Examples include loss of medical gas supply or a medical gas zone alarm, sewage backup in a patient care area, major water leak, loss of power to critical branch circuits, fire protection impairment, and loss of pressure in an occupied airborne infection isolation room. P1 work orders are dispatched immediately to the qualified trade at any hour.

## Definition of P2 Urgent work orders

A P2 Urgent is a condition that impairs patient care or department operations but does not present an immediate life safety threat, or that will become an emergency if not corrected promptly. Examples include an inoperable patient toilet or flush valve that requires the room to be taken out of service, a single inoperable medical gas outlet where the patient can be relocated or a portable source used temporarily, hot water outside the safe temperature range at a patient sink, a failed backflow preventer test, and loss of heating or cooling to a patient room.

## Definition of P3 Routine and P4 Scheduled work orders

A P3 Routine is a condition that should be corrected but does not affect patient care operations, such as a dripping faucet, a stained ceiling tile, a slow drain, or minor door hardware issues. A P4 Scheduled work order is preventive maintenance, inspection, or testing generated from the PM calendar, such as filter changes, generator load tests, and backflow annual testing.

## Measuring compliance with work order response and completion targets

Facilities Operations reports compliance with response and completion targets monthly by priority, trade, campus, and building at the Facilities Operations monthly review. Time spent awaiting parts is included in completion time and is not excluded from compliance reporting; work orders on hold for parts must carry the "Awaiting Parts" status with vendor and expected date documented. Work orders that exceed their completion target are escalated to the trade supervisor at 100% of target and to the Director of Facilities Operations at 200% of target.

## Interaction of FAC-POL-002 with after-hours dispatch

After-hours routing of work orders by priority is governed by SOP FAC-OPS-014, After-Hours Facilities Work Order Dispatch. The response targets in this policy apply regardless of time of day; the response clock starts when the work order is created in the CMMS.
