# Aqualine SV-200 Sensor Flush Valve Service Manual (Excerpt)

## Scope of the Aqualine SV-200 service manual excerpt

This excerpt covers troubleshooting and repair of the Aqualine SV-200 sensor-operated flush valve as installed on water closets throughout North Campus Tower B at Lakeshore Health. It is intended for licensed plumbers trained on the SV-200. General Maintenance technicians are not authorized to perform SV-200 flushometer or sensor valve repairs under SOP FAC-OPS-014. Refer to the full manufacturer manual for installation and commissioning.

## Aqualine SV-200 sensor flush valve components

The Aqualine SV-200 consists of a chrome valve body, a control stop with integral strainer, a diaphragm kit, a sensor and control module (the "sensor eye"), a sealed solenoid module, a hardwired 24 VAC power supply or battery pack, and a manual override button. The solenoid module is a proprietary sealed assembly specific to the SV-200. **There is no generic or cross-manufacturer substitute for the SV-200 solenoid module**; non-Aqualine solenoids will not mate to the valve body or respond to the sensor control signal, and their use voids the fixture warranty.

## SV-200 sensor indicator light status codes

The sensor window contains an indicator LED that communicates status:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Single green blink on approach | Normal; user detected |
| No light | No power (check battery or 24 VAC supply) |
| Slow red blink (1 per 2 s) | Low battery or low supply voltage |
| Rapid red blink, valve does not flush | Solenoid fault or solenoid not responding |
| Continuous red | Sensor obstructed or sensor fault |

## Troubleshooting a red blinking SV-200 sensor flush valve

When an SV-200 sensor flush valve shows a red blinking indicator and does not flush:

1. Verify the area is safe and the fixture is not overflowing. Close the control stop if water is running continuously.
2. Clean the sensor window with a soft damp cloth. Remove any tape, labels, or obstructions.
3. Check supply voltage at the module connector (24 VAC nominal) or replace batteries on battery models. A slow red blink that clears after battery replacement indicates a power issue only.
4. Press the manual override button. If the valve flushes with the override but not with the sensor, the sensor module is likely faulty. If the valve does not flush with the override, proceed to solenoid diagnosis.
5. A rapid red blink that persists after power is confirmed indicates a solenoid fault. Replace the solenoid module per the procedure below.

## Aqualine SV-200 solenoid module replacement procedure

1. Close the control stop and press the override to relieve pressure.
2. Disconnect power at the module connector.
3. Remove the top cover using the Aqualine security tool.
4. Unthread the solenoid module from the valve body by hand; do not use pliers on the solenoid housing.
5. Inspect the solenoid port and pilot orifice for debris. Clean with water only.
6. Install the new Aqualine SV-200 solenoid module (Aqualine part SV200-SOL) with the new O-ring supplied.
7. Reconnect power, open the control stop, and verify three consecutive sensor-activated flushes.

## Aqualine SV-200 diaphragm kit replacement procedure

Symptoms of a worn or fouled diaphragm include a continuous run-on after flush, short flushes, or a weak flush. To replace the SV-200 diaphragm:

1. Close the control stop and relieve pressure.
2. Remove the top cover and inner cover.
3. Lift out the diaphragm assembly and inspect the bypass orifice.
4. Install the new Aqualine SV-200 diaphragm kit (Aqualine part SV200-DK). Generic flushometer diaphragms are not dimensionally compatible with the SV-200 relief valve seat.
5. Reassemble, open the control stop, and verify flush volume.

## Aqualine SV-200 parts availability and stocking recommendation

Aqualine recommends that facilities with more than 50 installed SV-200 valves maintain on-site spares of solenoid modules and diaphragm kits equal to at least 5% of the installed base, because these parts are manufactured in batches and distributor stock is limited. Aqualine does not distribute through general building supply channels. A fixture with a failed solenoid module and no spare available must remain out of service until the replacement part arrives.
