# Lakeshore Health Campus and Building Facilities Reference

## Purpose of the Lakeshore Health campus facilities reference

This reference describes the campuses and buildings maintained by Lakeshore Health Facilities Operations, the general age and construction of each building, and the plumbing fixture standards installed in each. It is intended for facilities technicians, supervisors, Supply Chain buyers, and capital planning staff who need to know which buildings use commodity fixtures and which require specialty, manufacturer-specific parts. It does not replace building-specific as-built drawings, which are maintained in the Facilities document management system.

## North Campus Tower B building profile

Tower B on North Campus opened in 2019 and is the main inpatient tower for Lakeshore Health. It has 12 floors and houses medical/surgical units, step-down units, and the intensive care units (Medical ICU, Surgical ICU, Cardiac ICU, and Neuro ICU). Tower B was designed to a higher infection prevention and water management standard than the older towers, and as a result it was built with specialty plumbing fixtures that differ from the rest of the Lakeshore Health building portfolio. Tower B is the only Lakeshore building where these specialty fixtures are the standard installation on every inpatient floor.

## Tower B specialty plumbing fixture standard

The following specialty fixtures were specified by the Tower B design team and installed throughout North Campus Tower B:

- **Aqualine SV-200 sensor flush valves** on every patient and staff toilet. The SV-200 uses a proprietary solenoid module and diaphragm kit; there is no generic or cross-manufacturer substitute.
- **Aqualine sensor faucets** at hand hygiene sinks, using a model-specific sensor faucet solenoid.
- **ThermaMix TMV-50 thermostatic mixing valves** controlling hot water delivery temperature at point-of-use and unit-level locations. These valves are part of the Lakeshore water management program for Legionella control.
- **MedGuard MG-4 medical gas zone valve boxes** and MedGuard MG-4 headwall outlets for oxygen, medical air, and vacuum. The O2 outlet latch assembly is model-specific to the MG-4 outlet body.
- **Reduced pressure zone (RPZ) backflow preventers** at domestic water, process water, and equipment connections, serviced with manufacturer RPZ backflow repair kits and relief valve assemblies.

Repair parts for these Tower B fixtures are not stocked by general building supply distributors and are not interchangeable with the commodity parts used in other buildings.

## Commodity plumbing fixtures in other Lakeshore buildings

All Lakeshore buildings other than Tower B use standard commodity plumbing fixtures: manual or standard-diaphragm flushometers, conventional two-handle or single-lever faucets with generic cartridges, and standard medical gas outlets from multiple manufacturers installed during various renovations. Parts for these fixtures are carried in campus shop stock and are available from most plumbing distributors, typically same-day or next-day. Tower A (1972) and Riverside Hospital (1965) have the oldest piping systems and the highest rate of leak and drain work orders, but their parts are commodity items.

## Tower B specialty parts supply and storage history

From the Tower B opening in 2019 until February 2026, specialty repair parts for Tower B fixtures were supplied by Great Lakes Plumbing Supply under a consignment arrangement, with consignment stock held on-site in the North Campus Central Plant storeroom (see Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement LH-SC-2019-044). Effective 2026-02-24, plumbing parts are supplied by ACME Mechanical Supply under agreement LH-SC-2026-017. Supply Chain is the owner of vendor agreements; Facilities Operations is the owner of fixture standards. Technicians should consult the current supply agreement for lead times before committing a repair date to a nursing unit.
