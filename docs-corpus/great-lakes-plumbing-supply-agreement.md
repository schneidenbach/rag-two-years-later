# Plumbing Parts Supply and Consignment Agreement between Lakeshore Health and Great Lakes Plumbing Supply

## Contract status of the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement

Contract status: **Expired.** The Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement LH-SC-2019-044 ended 2026-02-23 and was not renewed. Plumbing parts supply transferred to ACME Mechanical Supply under agreement LH-SC-2026-017, effective 2026-02-24, as part of the Supply Chain vendor consolidation. This document is retained for reference and audit purposes. The closeout record appears in the final section.

## §1 Parties, term, and scope of the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement

§1.1 This agreement is between Lakeshore Health ("Lakeshore") and Great Lakes Plumbing Supply ("Great Lakes").

§1.2 The agreement became effective 2019-03-01, aligned with the commissioning of North Campus Tower B, and was amended in 2022 (Revision 2) to extend the term through 2026-02-23.

§1.3 Great Lakes supplied plumbing repair parts and fixtures for all Lakeshore campuses, including the specialty plumbing fixtures installed in North Campus Tower B.

## §2 Delivery commitments under the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement

§2.1 Great Lakes delivered standard catalog items to the designated campus receiving dock on the next Business Day following order receipt.

§2.2 For urgent orders, Great Lakes provided same-day courier delivery to North Campus within the Great Lakes regional service zone at no additional charge for up to four orders per month.

§2.3 In practice, typical delivery for plumbing parts under this agreement was 24 hours from order placement, and Tower B specialty parts were usually available immediately from on-site consignment stock (§3).

## §3 Consignment stock of Tower B specialty parts under the Great Lakes agreement

§3.1 Great Lakes maintained **consignment stock** of North Campus Tower B specialty plumbing parts on-site at North Campus, in a locked consignment cage within the Central Plant storeroom. Inventory in the cage remained the property of Great Lakes until withdrawn by Lakeshore.

§3.2 The consignment stock list was jointly set by Great Lakes and the Lakeshore plumbing supervisor and included, at minimum:

- Aqualine SV-200 sensor flush valve solenoid modules, diaphragm kits, and complete assemblies
- Aqualine sensor faucet solenoids
- ThermaMix TMV-50 thermostatic mixing valves and rebuild kits
- MedGuard MG-4 medical gas zone valves, O2 outlet latch assemblies, and vacuum outlet rebuild kits
- RPZ backflow repair kits and relief valve assemblies in sizes used in Tower B

§3.3 Lakeshore technicians withdrew consignment parts as needed, logging each withdrawal against the work order in the computerized maintenance management system (CMMS). Lakeshore was invoiced only upon withdrawal.

§3.4 Great Lakes performed a weekly cycle count and replenished the cage to agreed par levels within two Business Days.

## §4 Pricing and invoicing under the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement

§4.1 Consignment items were priced at the Great Lakes contract price list, which included a consignment carrying charge built into unit price.

§4.2 Invoices were issued monthly for all withdrawals and direct orders.

## §5 Service reporting under the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement

§5.1 Great Lakes provided a monthly report of consignment withdrawals, par level exceptions, and direct-order delivery performance to Lakeshore Supply Chain and the Facilities Operations plumbing supervisor.

§5.2 Facilities Operations participated in the annual review of the consignment stock list to reflect changes in Tower B fixture failure patterns.

## §6 Termination and removal of consignment stock under the Great Lakes agreement

§6.1 Upon expiration or termination, Great Lakes would remove all unwithdrawn consignment stock from Lakeshore premises within five Business Days, unless Lakeshore elected to purchase the remaining inventory at contract price.

## Closeout record for the Great Lakes Plumbing Supply agreement (2026-02-23)

Closeout of agreement LH-SC-2019-044 was completed 2026-02-23. Supply Chain issued notice of non-renewal following the decision to consolidate plumbing and mechanical parts supply under ACME Mechanical Supply. Lakeshore elected not to purchase the remaining consignment inventory. Great Lakes removed the North Campus consignment cage contents on 2026-02-23 and the cage was emptied. With this closeout, Lakeshore no longer holds on-site consignment stock of Tower B specialty parts. The successor agreement, ACME Mechanical Supply LH-SC-2026-017, does not include consignment or on-site stock (ACME agreement §4.3).
